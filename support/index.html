Support

Support, refunds, licences and privacy

If a file isn't working

  1. Open the Troubleshooting PDF in your download — it covers the common problems (Google Sheets import, example rows, a figure that looks wrong).
  2. Check the first-open figure on the product's page. If it matches, the file is working and the difference is in the data typed in.
  3. Still stuck? Message us through the Quidova store on Payhip (Contact). A person replies.

Refunds

If something doesn't work the way its page says, we'll fix it, send a corrected copy, or refund you. That covers genuine faults. It isn't a custom-build service.

Licences

The Couples Budget, The ADHD Life System: personal use — you and the people in your household. Change anything, keep backups. Please don't resell, share, give away or publish the files, or put them in a bundle or template library.

The Quote Builder: business use — run your own business with it and send clients the quotes it produces. The file itself is licensed to you alone.

The free calculators on this site: the code is open source under the MIT licence. The product images and text are © Quidova.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The calculators work entirely in your browser; nothing you type is stored or sent. They keep a short event log inside the page (for example “calculation completed”) so that analytics could be connected later; today that log goes nowhere. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security (GitHub's note on data collection). Purchases happen on Payhip, under Payhip's own privacy policy.

Not advice

These are tracking and calculating tools. They are not financial, tax, legal or medical advice.
