The one idea that makes it work

50/50 of everything isn't 50/50 of what you shared

In the example, a £14.50 cinema ticket was just for one person. Mark it Shared and the answer is £14.65. Mark it Personal — which it is — and the real answer is £7.40. Same four spends, one tag changed, half the number.

How the sum works

Only shared spends count. Each person's fair share is half of the shared total. Whoever paid more than half is owed the difference: (what one person paid on shared costs − what the other paid) ÷ 2.

Switch to by income and each person's share of the shared total is their take-home divided by both take-homes together — the same sum as the Couples Budget's Fair Split tab. In the example, on £2,100 and £1,400 take-home, Partner A's share is 60%, so Partner A owes Partner B £4.80 instead of being owed £7.40.
