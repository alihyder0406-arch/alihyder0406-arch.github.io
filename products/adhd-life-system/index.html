Why it exists

The ADHD Life System — Planner + Journal

Most planners assume a perfect day. Miss a few and they turn into a list of what you didn't do. This one is built the other way round: a day is one main thing and two small ones, missed days don't reset anything, and the Dashboard fills itself in from whatever you did manage to add.

Dashboard

Money in, left to spend, bills still to pay, tasks and habit ticks — each with one plain line saying what it means.

Daily Plan

One main thing, two small things.

Brain Dump

Get it out of your head; give each item a status, the energy it needs and one tiny next step.

Habits

Done % counts only the days so far, and there is no streak to break.

Money

Income, a budget per category, bills and a spending log, in £.

Weekly Reset

Five minutes once a week: what you planned and finished fills itself in.

How it works

Start in minutes

  1. Money — type your income and a rough budget over the example rows.
  2. Daily Plan — pick today's one main thing. Leave the rest if you like.
  3. Dashboard — glance at it. It totals itself from whatever you added.

First-open check: on the Dashboard, “Left to spend” should read £771.30 (the worked example).
