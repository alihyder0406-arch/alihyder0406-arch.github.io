Why it exists

The Couples Budget — Who Owes Who

When two people share costs from different cards, nobody quite knows the running total — so it turns into a conversation. This spreadsheet keeps the running total for you: who paid, what was shared, what was personal, and who owes who.

Tested in Microsoft Excel for Windows (desktop) and Google Sheets in a desktop browser (free — File → Import). Not tested in Excel for Mac, Excel on iPad or iPhone, Apple Numbers, LibreOffice or the Google Sheets mobile app. It may well work there — we have not checked, so we do not claim it.

Please read before buying

What it doesn't do

  • It does not connect to your bank. You type your own spending in.
  • It does not send reminders or notifications.
  • The Dashboard settles up 50/50. The income split is on the separate Fair Split tab — the Dashboard does not change to match it.
  • It totals everything you log. It does not filter by month.
  • It ships with “Partner A” and “Partner B” — renaming is manual, about a minute.
