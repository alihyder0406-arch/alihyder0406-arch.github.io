Why it exists

You enter your own rates once. It then prices every job from those rates, builds a quote you can send, and — after the work is done — tells you what that job really paid you per hour once materials and the extra time came out.

How it works

Start in minutes

  1. Open Your Rates and enter your hourly rate, minimum charge, travel and materials handling.
  2. Put your services on Service List with how long each actually takes you.
  3. Build a quote. Send the Client Quote tab.
  4. After the job, log the real hours. Read what you actually earned.

First-open check: on the Quote Builder tab the quote total should read £834.30 (the worked example).

Who it's for

Sole traders and very small service businesses who quote per job — cleaners, gardeners, handypeople, window cleaners, decorators, tutors, mobile groomers. Currency in £, UK dates.

Where it works — tested, not assumed

Tested in Microsoft Excel for Windows (desktop) and Google Sheets in a desktop browser (free — File → Import). Not tested in Excel for Mac, Excel on iPad or iPhone, Apple Numbers, LibreOffice or the Google Sheets mobile app. It may well work there — we have not checked, so we do not claim it.

Please read before buying

What it doesn't do

  • It does not tell you what to charge — you enter your own rates; it does the arithmetic.
  • It does not calculate tax, VAT or National Insurance, and it is not accounting or financial advice.
  • The Dashboard is narrow enough for a phone screen, but phones have not been tested; the wider tables need sideways scrolling.
